# Certification notes for version 0.1.1.0

Product: Qinglan - Path of Ascension (`9NGLSM7P5CMB`).

This update corrects objective tracking and room progression. The main
objective stays visible after conversations and reloads, and a gold marker
shows the next destination. Training guidance follows the actions still
required. Finished instruction panels close so the player can see the scene.
Walking through a room gateway starts the next room through the same rules
as interacting with it; combat and blessing choices retain their normal gates.

The game is free, offline and single-player. No account or server connection
is required. There are no advertisements, subscriptions or in-app purchases.

## Primary gameplay review route

1. Launch the game. Select an empty profile with Up/Down and Enter, or click
   the profile row. A gamepad uses the directional pad and A to confirm.
2. Follow the gold marker to the courtyard bell. Approach and press E (Y on
   a gamepad). E/Y advances conversation lines. Select a response with
   Up/Down and Enter/A, or click the response.
3. Follow the marker to the training station and finish its instructions.
   Face the practice post and land three sword hits using the left mouse
   button or J (X on a gamepad). Move close to the post if a swing misses.
4. Dodge once with Space/A. Cast Jade Sword Arc with 1/directional pad Up,
   then Flowing Ward with 2/directional pad Right. Allow each action to
   finish. Follow the marker to the sanctuary shrine and interact to recover.
5. Finish the route-letter conversation. Read the marked physical letter
   with E/Y; Esc/Menu closes its record viewer. Go to the marked bamboo gate
   and interact to confirm departure, then interact to enter Bamboo Hollow.
6. Read the marked water marks and delivery note after the entrance
   conversation. Proceed through the marked gateways to the two guided
   encounters, defeat the enemies, and select a blessing when offered.
7. Test the marked inspection shutter: interact to open it, close the record
   viewer, and interact again to close it. Continue to the third-room shrine
   and interact with the marked return station to return to the sanctuary.

Movement uses WASD/left stick; the mouse/right stick controls the camera.
Esc/Menu opens or closes menus. English and Simplified Chinese are available
in Settings. Progress uses local profile saves. Support:
zhiqizhangg@gmail.com.
