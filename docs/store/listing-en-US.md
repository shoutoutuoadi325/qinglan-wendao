## Description

Enter a world of mountain sanctuaries, elemental techniques, and Heavenly
Tribulations in Qinglan - Path of Ascension, a free offline single-player 3D
cultivation action game.

Combine sword attacks, dodges, techniques, and talismans as you explore
mysterious realms and advance your cultivation. Return to the sanctuary to
prepare for your next expedition, uncover the stories of the people you meet,
and choose your path through the campaign.

Play in English or Simplified Chinese, with local save profiles, keyboard and
mouse or gamepad controls, and adjustable graphics settings. No account,
advertisements, subscriptions, or in-app purchases.

## Features

- Offline single-player 3D cultivation adventure
- Sword combat, elemental techniques, talismans, and breakthrough trials
- English and Simplified Chinese, with local save profiles
- Free to play, with no advertisements or in-app purchases
