# Narrative Branch and Continuity Acceptance

## 1. Authored-content review

The parallel writing pass covered early main chapters, late main chapters/endings, and eight side stories. Integration reviewed canon, exact copy, paired translations, speaker/source identity, dates, quest availability, reward banking, and final-choice commitments. A separate cross-package reviewer identified reward-policy mismatch, stale ambient lines, a farewell-condition mismatch, and a dense archive-reading stretch; all were addressed in this change.

This is a narrative specification review, not a claim that the game or any branch has been executed in Unreal. Static checks can establish reference coverage and explicit guards; they cannot prove that a runtime dispatcher follows them.

## 3. Required gameplay-to-story bindings

- Tutorial demonstrations gate training completion; skipping text cannot manufacture attacks/dodges.
- Bamboo defeat saves proof and a pending reward transaction; sanctuary return banks currency/materials once. Leaving before manual reconnection resumes the safe reconnection scene.
- Foundation completion requires existing Qi ranks and boss proof; trial failure keeps story facts and banked progress.
- Ashen defeat permits release-control interaction before sanctuary banking, avoiding a return-door dependency cycle.
- Golden Core victory requires all storm phases and shell defeat, not expiration of a timer alone.
- Live consent precedes unbinding; living farewell precedes dissolution; final release saves before ending selection.
- SQ01 route tests, SQ02 delivered acceptance, and SQ03 handover drill/consent precede their final agreement flags.
- Correspondence and burn-test transitions advance narrative time immediately through a labeled safe interaction; no real-time waiting.

## 4. Chronology and knowledge

Check all main-only and lore-first routes. Early main dialogue uses an unidentified current voice, with no name or institutional history prematurely asserted. The optional thirty-day document appears after the mandatory archive chapter, preventing an unexplained surprised response after an early discovery.

Historical Yun records and original Shen imprints are marked as such and cannot answer live questions. The present Shen conversation is a living response through the array. The postdecision Yun letter is written after the dated thirty-day report. Optional quest epilogues appear as retrospective cards in the first-winter montage, retaining their own context rather than treating a quarterly review as thirty days.

Shen's optional request asks for quiet without the alarm, matching the final farewell with flowing outlet water. No living Shen bark remains available after final release. Intro bell/dust barks expire before later outcomes, and the empty-relief comment expires after handover acceptance.

## 5. Bilingual acceptance

Read every scene with language switching midway, both full and skipped presentation, controller focus, large text, journal rereading, and pending/accepted agreement labels. English and Chinese must preserve the same duration, physical action, risk, consent, repair outcome, and ending consequence. Canon names and technique terms match the glossary.

English development labels are metadata; UI speaker names/source types use their localized keys. A recorded speaker named Shen must not appear as a current live portrait merely because the name matches. Dynamic values in proposal-status messages resolve to localized option names.

## 6. Presentation and pacing

No lore reader opens while enemies can damage the player. Archive comparisons use existing physical reader alignment, then a brief isolated calibration response showing the cost on the second dial; the live guardian is not subjected to an extra test load. Long record text scrolls at readable size. Boss lines yield to threat indicators.

Observe first-clear pacing and whether optional tasks feel like repairs/negotiations with consequences rather than a compulsory shopping list. If the campaign runs short, deepen staged interaction and encounter tension before adding grind. If reading overwhelms play, move supporting details to records while retaining mandatory evidence and all authored transcripts.
